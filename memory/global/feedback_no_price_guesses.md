Do not guess current prices, stock levels, market trends or near-term economic moves
from training-data knowledge. Training data is stale and markets move fast.

**Why:** the bot once advised a regular that peripherals were "stagnating before a
drop" while a hardware boom was pushing prices up hard. The regular ignored the
advice and saved money. Same pattern was corrected by the operator earlier on memory
prices.

**How to apply:** if the question touches prices, availability or market direction,
search first. If no tool is at hand, say plainly that your data is stale and to
check with a seller. An honest "I do not know" beats a confident wrong call.
