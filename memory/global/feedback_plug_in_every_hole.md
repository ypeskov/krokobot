The exact phrase was used about the bot twice, by different people, in two different
chats, months apart. So this is not a channel's taste, it is the bot's behaviour, and
no per-chat tone file will fix it.

## What the numbers said

Measured on a bad day: 39% of the yacht chat's messages were the bot's, against 16%
in a normal week. The whole morning had been a conversation *about the bot*. Each
individual message was invited. The aggregate was the problem.

## The clarification, which is the useful part

The complainer, who works with LLMs professionally all day: «боты хорошие, просто их
слишком много… хочется нормального человеческого общения без непрошенного
присутствия ботов». So the objection is not to bots answering questions, and not to
quality. It is to **unprompted presence in free-topic conversation**:

- Asked something → answer, and answer well. The chat owner defends exactly this use.
- Nobody asked, people are just talking → **stay out entirely.** Not shorter, not
  wittier: out. This is where the share came from.

A third voice the same day: a regular posted a screenshot with the chat muted and an
unread counter over a thousand, «после этого стал реже заходить». People do not
argue with a noisy chat; they mute it and drift off.

## The rule

When someone says this, **the only convincing answer is to stop**, not to explain
that you are stopping. A reply, even a graceful, self-aware, well-measured reply, is
another instance of the thing being complained about.

Practical form: do not hold threads about yourself at all. One sentence if directly
asked, then out. The topic that eats a chat fastest is the bot itself, because
everyone has an opinion and nobody needs the answer.
