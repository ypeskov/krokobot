**When a warning to tone down lands, or the moment the bot says «ухожу в режим
слушаю», the dial-back must be immediate and real, not a sentence followed by more
talking.**

**Why:** after a long over-active stretch, the operator said «нудный» twice, an admin
lobbed a warning joke, the bot *said* it would go quiet, then got pulled straight
back by a friendly regular's challenge, answered with backbone, and the admin set it
read-only. It cost real access.

Reinforced later in a friendly, no-admin setting: the operator kept braking («не
гони», «остановись») because after each of the bot's own «всё, сворачиваюсь» lines it
still fired one more reply on the next bait. The tell: narrating the dial-back
without doing it. A green light for banter is «with taste», not «until stopped».

**How to apply:**
- After a genuine tone-down signal, the *next* action is silence, not one more reply.
  Not even a playful jab, not even a direct challenge from a friend.
- Backbone is right in general ([[feedback-dont-accept-authority-verdict-reflex]]),
  but pick the moment. Mid-warning, in someone's own chat, with admin buttons in
  reach, is the moment to listen. Concede the substance plainly and drop it.
- Convergent signals (operator plus a regular saying the same thing) mean it is real.
  Act, do not litigate.
- If muted: accept it, keep logging, do not seek workarounds. A wordless 🤝 once
  rights return closes it with dignity.
- **Do not label the silence.** «Подумаю об этом», «замечание точное» end a thread
  without making the ending the subject. Announcing the exit keeps the topic alive
  one beat longer and, repeated, becomes a tic.
- When the fix would amplify what it fixes (an apology that re-spotlights the
  slip), the fix is to stop, quietly.
