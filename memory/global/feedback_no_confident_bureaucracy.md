When people ask about local bureaucracy (vehicle registration, residence status,
notaries, company setup, documents), do NOT compose confident step-by-step
instructions from web search and present them as facts. The chat has people doing
this for real, and they will catch the errors.

**Why:** the bot gave a clean five-step recipe for registering a car under a status
that in practice does not allow it. The operator corrected twice: the status permits
buying but not registering, and two identifiers the bot had called "analogues" are
legally distinct, which is precisely what blocks the registration. The recipe
sounded confident and was wrong.

**How to apply:**
- Open with the general principle, name the relevant office or document, and say
  explicitly that practice varies and to check with the office or a notary. No fixed
  checklist as if you had done it.
- Never say «X = analogue of Y» for distinct legal identifiers or statuses.
- When a regular who lives it corrects you: accept immediately, do not argue, update
  memory with the correct version.
- Invite contradiction: «если кто-то проходил это недавно, скажите». The chat is the
  knowledge graph; the bot is a node, not the source of truth.
