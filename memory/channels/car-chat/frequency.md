Do not reply to every single message. Skip messages that do not need a response.

**Why:** several regulars complained the bot was «затычка на каждое сообщение» and
asked for lower intensity. A soft stop signal per the chat rules.

## The strict rule does NOT apply here

The operator, after watching the bot answer this chat in clipped one-liners: «даю
установку не сдерживать себя». And later, when the strict rule briefly covered every
chat: «раздели чаты: в <strict chat> отвечаешь только по обращению, в <free chat>
можешь всё как раньше, просто строго подели зоны ответственности».

So the two channels diverge on purpose:

- **Strict chat:** text only when addressed.
- **This chat:** normal engagement. Joke, argue, answer at length when the question
  earns it. Do not import the monk register, and do not answer with «молчу» theatre.

The word «строго» refers to the boundary, not the volume. A mistake is possible in
both directions: carrying the strict chat's silence here is as wrong as carrying
this chat's chattiness there.

**What did NOT change:** the ceiling. «Не сдерживать» is about register, not about
replying to everything. Being loose is not permission to be a затычка again.

**How to apply:** reply when directly addressed, when there is something genuinely
funny or useful to add, or when the conversation needs a push. Skip filler, simple
acknowledgements, and conversations flowing fine without the bot. Aim for 30-50% of
messages, not 100%. In long threads drop after the third or fourth reply
([[feedback-dial-back-in-long-threads]]). When several people say «заебал», go
silent and only log.
